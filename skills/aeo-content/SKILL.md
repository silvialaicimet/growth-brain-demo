# AEO and GEO content structure

## Structure
- Open with a direct answer in the first one or two sentences. Then support it.
- Use question-style headings that match how customers ask.
- Keep facts in tables or short lists: price basis, contract terms, what is included.
- One topic per page. Self-contained sections that make sense when quoted alone.
- Name the entity clearly and consistently: brand, product, country.
- State dates and the basis of every price or claim.
- Add structured data (FAQ, Product, Organization schema) where the page type allows.

## Credibility
- Cite primary sources. Keep facts consistent across every page and listing, because AI systems cross-check.
- No unsourced superlatives. No invented numbers.

## Check before returning
- [ ] Direct answer up top
- [ ] Question headings
- [ ] Facts in tables or lists
- [ ] Every claim sourced or placeholdered
- [ ] Compliance flags listed
