# Growth budget

## Read first

`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/context/budget/budget-structure.md`: the budget by category and quarter, the experimentation fund rules and the open questions. In this demo it is a placeholder that shows the shape only.

## How to answer

- **Quote, never recalculate silently.** Give the figure, the file and its date. If you compute something, show the arithmetic.
- **Say it is a draft.** Budgets change after agency calls and reviews. Newer figures from the owner override the file.
- **Spend approval:** follow the rules in the file. The owner of a line proposes, the head of the function approves in the weekly meeting, and leadership is informed above the threshold the file sets.
- **Never invent a line or a number.** If a cost is not in the file, say so.

## Not in this plugin

People costs, salaries, headcount and hiring. Send those questions to the plugin owner.
